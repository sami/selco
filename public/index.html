From tape measure to trade counter

1. Get the sizes Room or wall sizes from the customer are enough to start. Waste allowances are already built in.

2. Check the drawing The plan redraws live. If it doesn't match what the customer described, you've caught it before anything's ordered.

3. Treat it as an estimate Copy the list into a quote as a starting point, then check quantities and pack sizes with the customer before ordering.

These figures are estimates, not exact quantities. They use standard coverage rates and can't allow for the customer's site, so check before ordering and send structural questions to someone qualified.
