5 order lines · 6 checklist itemsTake this list to your local branch or Click & Deliver
  • Acclimatise packs flat in the room for 48 hours before laying.
  • Stagger end joints at least 300 mm row to row; never let joints line up.
  • Plank thickness (8 mm) doesn't change how much floor you buy — it sets the threshold-bar height and how far you undercut the door casings.
  • 10 mm expansion gap at every wall and fixed point; the floating floor needs room to move.
  • Quantities are estimates, verify product coverage and site conditions before ordering.
