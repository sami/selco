  • Posts go 750 mm into the ground (deeper than usual because a 6 ft fence catches a lot of wind). You need a 2.58 m post, so buy the 2.7 m stocked length. Check for services before digging.
  • Concrete posts and gravel boards keep all timber clear of soil. Panels drop into the slots, no fixings needed.
